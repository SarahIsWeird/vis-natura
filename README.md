Vis Natura is a Minecraft 1.21.5 nature-themed magic mod made for the
[Mod Garden: Nature](https://modgarden.net/events/mod-garden/nature) mod jam. The goal is to harness the power
of the nature around you and put it to good use for your own good.

It'll take some time until this README is expanded, mainly because the mod is still heavily in development and
many things might change at any minute.

## The idea

Luscious forests, places too hot to sleep, barren islands floating in the void, life can be found everywhere,
even in the most impossible places. How can the flora and fauna survive in all those places?

Recently you've stumbled upon an artifact that may offer answers: a green crystal atop lying next to a wilted
flower. It's pulsating with a raw energy unlike anything you've seen before. You move to pick it up, but it
brushes against the wilted flower, exploding into a small cloud of green dust. It lingers in the air for a
second, until it is absorbed by the flower, turning it healthy again. Maybe you can find a way to use this
mysterious energy for your own good?
